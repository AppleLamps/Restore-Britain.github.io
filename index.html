📈 Analysis

Right-Wing Vote Split

The first poll featuring Restore Britain shows a significant split in the right-wing vote. With Rupert Lowe's party on the ballot:

  • Reform UK drops to 25% (from 30%+ in other polls)
  • Conservatives fall to 13% (from high-teens/low-twenties)
  • Restore Britain captures 10% immediately

Key Takeaways

• Immediate Impact: Restore Britain polling at 10% in its first survey suggests significant potential traction.

• Reform UK Hit: The 5-point drop for Reform when Restore Britain is on the ballot indicates direct competition for the same voter base.

• Methodology Note: Find Out Now consistently polls Reform higher than other pollsters. The impact may be even more pronounced in other surveys.

Councillor Defections

Restore Britain has gained 9 councillors since launch (7 from Kent, 2 from Warwickshire), though 1 subsequently left. The party is not yet officially registered, meaning defectors must sit as independents.
