📰 Recent Developments

February 24, 2026
Merger talks with Advance UK collapse — Ben Habib issues a nine-minute video statement denouncing Rupert Lowe, calling him "owner and dictator" of Restore Britain. Habib criticizes the lack of a draft constitution and claims Restore has "no clarity" on leadership accountability. The rupture signals competing factions on the populist right cannot unite.
February 23, 2026
Darren Rance quits Restore Britain and reapplies to Reform UK — The North Northamptonshire councillor (Brickhill and Queensway ward, Wellingborough), who defected just days earlier, announced he was leaving Restore Britain after "only a day to work out that Restore does not stand for the values I believe in." He apologized to Reform colleagues and described the party as "far more sinister than I first thought."
February 20, 2026
Leicestershire councillor Charles Whitford defects to Restore Britain after "clashes" with Reform UK county council leader Dan Harrison. Whitford had been suspended from the cabinet following complaints about emails he sent to residents regarding immigration.
February 20, 2026
Two Warwickshire councillors defect: Scott Cameron and Luke Cooper leave Reform UK for Restore Britain. Reform's group leader George Finch removed the whip from both, accusing them of "indulging in stunts."
February 19, 2026
Seven Kent County Council councillors defect: Paul Thomas, Oliver Bradshaw, Brian Black, Robert Ford, Isabella Kemp, Maxine Fothergill, and Dean Burns join Restore Britain. Fellow ex-Reform councillor Bill Barrett declined, stating "Restore is too right-wing for my political appetite."
