⚡ Right-Wing Vote Split

Restore Britain's presence has created a persistent split in the right-wing vote:

  • Reform UK: 24-25% (down from 30%+)
  • Conservatives: 16-17%
  • Restore Britain: 7-10% (stabilizing around 7%)

Poll of Polls Average: 9% Restore Britain, 26% Reform UK, 15% Labour, 14% Conservative

⚠️ Merger Collapse

Talks between Advance UK and Restore Britain collapsed in February:

  • Ben Habib called Lowe "owner and dictator"
  • No draft constitution or party structure published
  • Right-wing consolidation efforts fracturing

Despite collapse, Restore Britain claims 100,000+ members

📰 Recent Developments

March 25, 2026
Latest poll shows 8% support — highest since launch — Find Out Now poll (2,948 sample) shows Restore Britain at 8%, up from 7% earlier in March. Reform UK at 25%, Greens at 19%. This is the fourth consecutive poll with Restore Britain as a registered party option, showing a potential upward trend.
March 4, 2026
Poll shows 7% support — Find Out Now poll (2,082 sample) shows Restore Britain at 7%, Reform UK at 24%, Greens at 20%. This is the third consecutive poll with Restore Britain as a registered party option.
February 24, 2026
Merger talks with Advance UK collapse — Ben Habib issues a nine-minute video statement denouncing Rupert Lowe, calling him "owner and dictator" of Restore Britain. Habib criticizes the lack of a draft constitution and claims Restore has "no clarity" on leadership accountability.
February 23, 2026
Darren Rance quits Restore Britain — The North Northamptonshire councillor (Brickhill and Queensway ward, Wellingborough), who defected just days earlier, announced he was leaving Restore Britain after "only a day to work out that Restore does not stand for the values I believe in." He reapplied to Reform UK.
February 20, 2026
Leicestershire councillor Charles Whitford defects to Restore Britain after "clashes" with Reform UK county council leader Dan Harrison. Whitford had been suspended from the cabinet following complaints about emails he sent to residents regarding immigration.
February 20, 2026
Two Warwickshire councillors defect: Scott Cameron and Luke Cooper leave Reform UK for Restore Britain. Reform's group leader George Finch removed the whip from both, accusing them of "indulging in stunts."
February 19, 2026
Seven Kent County Council councillors defect: Paul Thomas, Oliver Bradshaw, Brian Black, Robert Ford, Isabella Kemp, Maxine Fothergill, and Dean Burns join Restore Britain. Fellow ex-Reform councillor Bill Barrett declined, stating "Restore is too right-wing for my political appetite."
February 18, 2026
Find Out Now releases national voting intention poll showing 'Other' at highest level ever recorded. The poll included Restore Britain as a registered party for the first time.
February 14, 2026
Rupert Lowe launches Restore Britain as a registered national political party after acrimonious split from Reform UK in March 2025. Party claims 50,000 members at launch.
