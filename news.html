Restore Britain Stabilizes at 7% in Latest Polling

The third consecutive poll featuring Restore Britain as a registered party shows support stabilizing around 7%. Find Out Now's March 3-4 survey of 2,082 GB adults places RB at 7%, with Reform UK at 24% and the Greens surging to 20%. The data suggests the party has established a core base following its February launch, though it remains below the 10% peak seen in pre-registration polling.

Seven Kent Councillors Defect, Form Official Group

Seven former Reform UK councillors have joined Restore Britain, instantly making it the third-largest group on Kent County Council. The defectors — Maxine Fothergill, Robert Ford, Paul Thomas, Dean Burns, Isabella Kemp, Brian Black, and Oliver Bradshaw — will form an official Restore Britain group. February 14, 2026

Restore Britain Launches as Registered Political Party

Rupert Lowe has formally launched Restore Britain as a registered national political party, five months after his suspension from Reform UK. The launch event at Great Yarmouth's Britannia Pier drew significant attention, with Lowe pledging to field hundreds of candidates at the next general election. The party claims 50,000 members at launch.
